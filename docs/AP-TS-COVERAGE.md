# AP and Telangana coverage checklist

Checked on 5 November 2026. This list shows what a student needs after Intermediate, and whether the
website has it. It may still miss things, so add to it whenever a gap is found.

**Status words**
- **On the site**: a record exists. It still waits for admin review before students can see it.
- **Partly**: something exists, but details are missing.
- **Missing**: not on the site yet.

**Rule:** a detail goes on the site only after it is read on an official page. News and coaching sites
can point to what to look for, but they are not the source.
